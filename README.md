# PartyPilot Forever

### Your dungeon preparation companion for World of Warcraft Forever

**PartyPilot Forever** is a World of Warcraft addon designed to help small groups of friends prepare for dungeon adventures together.

Instead of replacing traditional quest trackers or dungeon databases, PartyPilot focuses on something different:

**Helping players understand what their group needs before entering a dungeon.**

The addon brings together dungeon information, quest progression, prerequisites, loot objectives, and shared interests in one interface.

## Features

### 🏰 Dungeon Exploration

Browse available dungeon information, including:

* Recommended levels.
* Known dungeon quests.
* Quest prerequisites and chains.
* Boss encounters.
* Documented loot and rewards.

### 📜 Group Preparation

Compare known quest progress and preparation requirements across multiple characters.

Identify completed quests, active objectives, missing prerequisites, and information that still needs verification.

### ⚔️ Bosses & Loot

Explore documented dungeon encounters and their associated rewards.

Track items you want to obtain and identify which rewards may interest other group members.

### 🎯 Loot Wishlist

Create and manage personal item objectives.

Connect desired items to documented dungeon and boss sources whenever reliable information is available.

### 🤝 Collective Objectives

Bring individual character objectives together to help the group prepare for dungeon runs.

PartyPilot is designed for friends who already communicate through voice chat and want a simple way to organize game information.

## World of Warcraft Forever

PartyPilot is being developed specifically for **World of Warcraft Forever**.

The project uses Blizzard's permitted in-game addon APIs and explores compatible community data sources.

Dungeon, quest, prerequisite, and loot information may be incomplete while Forever databases are being updated and verified.

PartyPilot aims to distinguish confirmed information from historical, community-sourced, and unknown data.

Some multiplayer features depend on Blizzard's addon communication restrictions and require validation in the live game.
